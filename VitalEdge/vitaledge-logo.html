05 — Usage Notes

Do

  • Use the mark primarily in Soft White (#F4F4F1) on dark backgrounds
  • Use the mark in Matte Black (#050505) on light backgrounds
  • Keep the Electric Blue accent on Facet 9's top sliver as the sole colour detail
  • Maintain wide letter-spacing in the wordmark at all times
  • Use "COLLECTIVE" in a noticeably smaller size than "VITALEDGE"
  • Allow generous clear space equal to the width of the mark around all lockups
  • Scale the SVG proportionally — never stretch or skew
  • Reserve accent colours (blue, emerald, gold) for single-facet highlights only

Do Not

  • Do not add drop shadows, bevels, emboss or any 3D effects
  • Do not use gradients across the full mark — flat facets only
  • Do not outline or stroke the mark
  • Do not place the mark on busy photographic backgrounds
  • Do not alter the geometry or proportion of any facet
  • Do not use more than one accent colour simultaneously
  • Do not compress letter-spacing in the wordmark
  • Do not use the wordmark alone without the mark in brand-primary contexts
  • Do not rotate or flip the mark
